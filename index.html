Reads ASL letters from your webcam

A Python program that finds your hand, reads the fingerspelled letter, and builds words. We tested it on people it had never seen.

How it works

  1. Find the hand

    MediaPipe locates 21 points on your hand in each camera frame.

  2. Clean the numbers

    The points are centered on the wrist and scaled by hand size.

  3. Guess the letter

    A scikit-learn Random Forest picks the most likely letter.

  4. Hold to confirm

    A letter counts after about half a second, then joins the word.

Which letters

24 letters are covered. J and Z are left out because they need motion.

ABCDEFGHIJKL MNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

What it can't do yet

  • It is an alphabet recognizer, not a sign language translator.
  • Only 2 people were tested, so these numbers are a small early result. We have not tested across a diverse group of people.
  • Everyone signed with the same hand, in similar camera conditions.
  • Only hand landmark numbers were saved. No photos or video of participants are stored.
