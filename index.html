ASL Alphabet Recognizer

Real-time fingerspelling recognition in Python, tested on people it has never seen. Built at Knight Hacks 2026.

Demo

Watch the demo video

What it does

A desktop program reads your webcam, finds your hand, and turns the ASL letters you sign into text. Hold a letter for about half a second and it gets added to a word.

How it works

  1. MediaPipe finds 21 points on the hand.
  2. The points are centered on the wrist and scaled by hand size.
  3. A scikit-learn Random Forest guesses the letter.
  4. A letter only counts after it is held steadily, which cuts down on flicker.

Results

We trained on some people and tested on others. The model is only scored on people it never saw during training.

TestAccuracy
Random split (frames from the same person mixed together)100.0%
Unseen person p0156.9%
Unseen person p0263.9%
Mean on unseen people (2 people)60.4%

The random split looks perfect because neighboring video frames are nearly identical, so the model has effectively seen the test answers. Testing on a new person gives the honest number. Live speed: about 30 frames per second.

Limits

  • This is an alphabet recognizer, not a sign language translator.
  • J and Z are not included because they involve motion.
  • Only 2 people were tested, so the numbers are a small early result and will change with more people. It has not been tested across a diverse group of people.
  • Only hand landmark numbers were saved. No photos or video of participants are stored.
